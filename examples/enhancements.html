Prepare before upload
JPEG/PNG preparation, up to 40 MiB and 40 megapixels. Output: WebP, at most 2560 pixels per side and 5 MiB. Existing WebP files pass through unchanged. The upload limit is 6 MiB. Preparation happens locally; nothing is sent until Upload is clicked.


    

    Shared editor hook
    The same preparation callback is applied before the editor upload callback. Animated formats are retained without conversion and must still meet the upload limit.
